Level editor

Terrain: drag to paint, Shift-drag fills a rectangle · Objects: click to place · right-click erases

Level

Each piece of gold adds 2 s. In a campaign the clock carries over from level to level (the campaign sets the starting time); this limit is used when the level is played on its own.

Size

Rows Columns

A solid border is added around the level. The game shows 10 columns across, so wider levels scroll sideways.
